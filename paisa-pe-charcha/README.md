## 🚀 Key Modules & Capabilities

### 1. 🤖 AI Financial Assistant Terminal
- **Interactive Command Center:** Full-screen toggle, multi-turn conversation memory, copy response, TXT transcript export, and chat clear controls.
- **Specialized Advisor Modes:**
  - *All-in-One Advisor*
  - *Tax Optimizer (Old vs New Regime FY 2024-25 / 2025-26)*
  - *Mutual Funds & SIP Wealth Coach*
  - *Sovereign Scheme Matcher*
- **Dual AI Engine:** High-performance built-in Indian Financial Reasoning Engine with zero dependencies, plus secure client integration for external Google Gemini APIs.

### 2. 🧮 10 Interactive Financial Calculators
1. **SIP Calculator:** Compound returns, rupee-cost averaging, wealth gain breakdown, and Chart.js donut visualization.
2. **Loan EMI Calculator:** Monthly EMI, total interest vs principal, and amortization breakdown.
3. **Compound Interest Calculator:** Quarterly, half-yearly, annual, and monthly compounding frequencies.
4. **Income Tax Calculator:** Side-by-side comparison of Section 115BAC New Regime (₹75k standard deduction + 87A rebate up to ₹7.75L) vs Old Regime with 80C, 80D, 24(b), and HRA.
5. **Fixed Deposit (FD) Calculator:** Standard Indian banking quarterly compounding.
6. **Recurring Deposit (RD) Calculator:** Post office and commercial bank monthly deposit compounding.
7. **Retirement Calculator:** Inflation-adjusted lifestyle expense projection and required monthly SIP.
8. **Inflation Calculator:** Purchasing power degradation over 1 to 40 years.
9. **Lumpsum Investment Calculator:** Power of CAGR for one-time capital investments.
10. **Salary (CTC to In-Hand) Calculator:** Detailed corporate Indian salary slip breakdown (Basic, HRA, Employee EPF, and Net In-Hand).

### 3. 📚 Indian Financial Knowledge Centre
- **RBI & Banking Safety:** Repo rate transmission, CRR/SLR, and the DICGC ₹5,00,000 deposit insurance per depositor.
- **SEBI & Capital Markets:** Investor protection charter, ASBA IPO bidding, and SCORES complaint redressal.
- **Income Tax Demystified:** Slabs, capital gains tax (LTCG at 12.5% above ₹1.25L, STCG at 20%), and deductions.
- **Mutual Funds:** Direct-Growth plans vs Regular distributor commission leakage.
- **Stock Markets:** Demat/Trading mechanics, BSE/NSE, and PE valuation ratios.
- **Personal Budgeting:** The 50/30/20 rule and building a 6-month liquid emergency corpus.
- **Digital Security & UPI:** The golden rule ("UPI PIN is never entered to receive money") and National Cyber Helpline **1930**.
- **Insurance Blueprint:** Pure term life insurance (15-20x annual income) vs avoiding low-yield ULIPs.

### 4. 🇮🇳 Government Schemes Directory
Direct links, interest rates, eligibility criteria, and sovereign guarantees for:
- **Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY):** 8.2% p.a. tax-free (EEE) for girls up to age 10.
- **Public Provident Fund (PPF):** 7.1% p.a. tax-free (EEE) with court attachment immunity.
- **National Pension System (NPS):** Section 80CCD(1B) exclusive ₹50,000 tax deduction.
- **Atal Pension Yojana (APY):** Guaranteed monthly pension of ₹1,000 to ₹5,000.
- **Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY):** Zero balance with ₹2 Lakh accidental cover.
- **PMJJBY & PMSBY:** ₹4 Lakh combined life and accidental cover for under ₹460/year.
- **Senior Citizen Savings Scheme (SCSS):** 8.2% p.a. quarterly payout up to ₹30 Lakhs.
- **Mahila Samman Savings Certificate (MSSC):** 7.5% p.a. 2-year sovereign certificate for women.

### 5. 📊 Market Insights Dashboard
- **Live-synced Benchmark Indices:** NIFTY 50, BSE SENSEX, NIFTY BANK, NIFTY IT.
- **Commodities & Currencies:** Gold 24K (per 10g), Silver (1kg), Brent Crude, USD/INR.
